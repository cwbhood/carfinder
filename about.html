Why CarFinder

Skip the fifty Google searches

“I wanted one page where someone who knows nothing about cars can see every new car on sale, what it lists for, what dealers really charge, and what it costs to drive off in their state. So I described that to Claude and we built it. Then I asked for India. Then nine more countries. Then motorcycles, boats and a photo of each.”Destin Jones

The whole project is a conversation turned into software: each request became a feature, each feature was tested on real quotes and on phone-sized screens, and the result is one self-contained page anyone can run, read or fork. The research behind every country's taxes and fees is in the repository, next to the code that applies it.

The method

How these are built

Open by default

Code, data, tax research notes, tests and design notes live in the repository. Anyone can read how a number was computed, and why.

Vibe coded with Claude

Claude does the research and the heavy lifting on code and content. A person sets the goals, reviews the work and owns the decisions, including what ships and when.

Small, testable steps

Every dataset row is validated, the pricing core is run for every car in every region, known real-world quotes are asserted, and the page is driven headlessly at phone and desktop widths before it ships.

Honest about the numbers

Dealer prices are bands, taxes follow the published rules on the compile date, exchange rates are dated, and each tab ends with a plain-language note on what was assumed. Wrong price? Open an issue.
