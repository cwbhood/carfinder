Methodology

How we calculate

Every number in CarFinder is a list price from a published source, a dealer band set per model, and a stack of taxes and fees from the rules of the region you pick. This page shows all of it: the basis per country, the bands, every fee table with its source, the exchange rates and their date, what is an estimate, and how to correct a figure.

It is generated by the build from the same data files the app ships with, so it cannot drift from the numbers, and the coverage figures on it are measured at every build. Car prices as of {{DATE_CARS}}, aircraft as of {{DATE_AIR}}, exchange rates of {{DATE_FX}}.
