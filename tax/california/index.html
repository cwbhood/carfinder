Car sales tax in California

California charges 7.25% to 11.75% sales tax on a car, depending on the city or county where you register it. Without a city CarFinder uses 8.75%. Trade-in credit: none. The trade-in allowance is part of the taxable selling price: tax is charged on the full price before the trade. Add a dealer doc fee (typically $85, capped by law) and about $350 for title, registration and plates.
