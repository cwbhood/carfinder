Car sales tax in Colorado

Colorado charges 7.8% sales tax on a car on average, state and local combined. 2.9% state plus local. Denver metro runs 8%-9%. Trade-in credit: yes. Colorado taxes the price less your trade-in. Add a dealer doc fee (typically $700) and about $350 for title, registration and plates.

Local taxes in Colorado

2.9% state plus local; Denver metro runs 8%-9%. CarFinder uses 7.8% as a typical combined rate for Colorado until its city and county table is added; your dealer will charge the rate for your address. California and New York already have every city and county.

Common questions

What is the car sales tax in Colorado?
Colorado charges 7.8% sales tax on a car on average, state and local combined. 2.9% state plus local. Denver metro runs 8%-9%.
Does Colorado give a trade-in tax credit?
Trade-in credit: yes. Colorado taxes the price less your trade-in. On a Toyota RAV4 a $15,000 trade-in saves about $1,170.
How much are dealer doc fees in Colorado?
Typically about $700; it is a dealer charge, so ask for it before you sign.
