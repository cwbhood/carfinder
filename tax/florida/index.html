Car sales tax in Florida

Florida charges 6% sales tax on a car on average, state and local combined. 6% state plus a county surtax (up to 1.5%) on the first $5,000. Trade-in credit: yes. Florida taxes the price less your trade-in. Add a dealer doc fee (typically $995) and about $350 for title, registration and plates.

Local taxes in Florida

6% state plus a county surtax (up to 1.5%) on the first $5,000; doc fees are among the highest. CarFinder uses 6% as a typical combined rate for Florida until its city and county table is added; your dealer will charge the rate for your address. California and New York already have every city and county.

Common questions

What is the car sales tax in Florida?
Florida charges 6% sales tax on a car on average, state and local combined. 6% state plus a county surtax (up to 1.5%) on the first $5,000.
Does Florida give a trade-in tax credit?
Trade-in credit: yes. Florida taxes the price less your trade-in. On a Toyota RAV4 a $15,000 trade-in saves about $900.
How much are dealer doc fees in Florida?
Typically about $995; it is a dealer charge, so ask for it before you sign.
