Car sales tax in Hawaii

Hawaii charges a 4.5% general excise tax on a car instead of sales tax. Trade-in credit: none. The general excise tax applies to the full price of the vehicle without considering the trade-in. Add a dealer doc fee (typically $300) and about $350 for title, registration and plates.

Local taxes in Hawaii

4% excise tax plus county surcharge (4.712% on Oahu). CarFinder uses 4.5% as a typical combined rate for Hawaii until its city and county table is added; your dealer will charge the rate for your address. California and New York already have every city and county.
