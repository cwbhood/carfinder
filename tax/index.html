Car sales tax
by state

Every state and DC taxes a car purchase differently: most charge sales tax, but Georgia charges a one-time TAVT, North Carolina a 3% highway use tax and South Carolina a fee capped at $500, while Alaska, Montana and New Hampshire charge nothing. 5 states tax the full price even when you trade a car in. Pick a state for its rate, its trade-in rule, a worked example and the sources.
