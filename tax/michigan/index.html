Car sales tax in Michigan

Michigan charges 6% sales tax on a car. Trade-in credit: up to $12,000. Michigan taxes the difference up to a cap that rises $1,000 every January: $12,000 in 2026, $13,000 in 2027, with no cap from 2029. Add a dealer doc fee (typically $290, capped by law) and about $350 for title, registration and plates.
