Car sales tax in Minnesota

Minnesota charges a 6.875% motor vehicle sales tax on a car instead of sales tax. Trade-in credit: yes. Minnesota taxes the price less your trade-in. Add a dealer doc fee (typically $125, capped by law) and about $350 for title, registration and plates.
