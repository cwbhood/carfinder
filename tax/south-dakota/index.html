Car sales tax in South Dakota

South Dakota charges a 4% motor vehicle excise tax on a car instead of sales tax. Trade-in credit: yes. South Dakota taxes the price less your trade-in. Add a dealer doc fee (typically $250) and about $350 for title, registration and plates.

Common questions

What is the car sales tax in South Dakota?

Does South Dakota give a trade-in tax credit?
Trade-in credit: yes. South Dakota taxes the price less your trade-in. On a Toyota RAV4 a $15,000 trade-in saves about $600.
