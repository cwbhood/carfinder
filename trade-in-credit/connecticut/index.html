Trade-in tax credit CT

Does Connecticut give a trade-in tax credit?

Yes. Connecticut taxes the price less the trade-in. On a $40,000 car, a $15,000 trade-in saves you about $953 in sales tax.

The trade-in still pays for part of the car in every state; the difference is only whether it also cuts the tax. To see it on a real car, enter your trade-in under Cost assumptions in CarFinder for Connecticut.
