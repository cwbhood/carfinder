Trade-in tax credit by state

42 states tax the price of a car less your trade-in. California, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Oregon and Virginia tax the full price, Michigan caps the credit at $12,000 in 2026, and Alaska, Montana and New Hampshire have no tax to reduce. On a $40,000 car with a $15,000 trade-in the difference is $900 to $1,500 in tax.
