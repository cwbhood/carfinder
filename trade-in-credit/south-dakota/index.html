Trade-in tax credit · SD

Does South Dakota give a trade-in tax credit?

Yes. South Dakota taxes the price less the trade-in. On a $40,000 car, a $15,000 trade-in saves you about $600 in motor vehicle excise tax.

The trade-in still pays for part of the car in every state; the difference is only whether it also cuts the tax. To see it on a real car, enter your trade-in under Cost assumptions in CarFinder for South Dakota.

States that tax the full price

Most states tax the price less the trade-in. These don’t, or cap the credit:

  • California: no
  • District of Columbia: no
  • Hawaii: no
  • Michigan: capped at $12,000
  • Oregon: no
  • Virginia: no
